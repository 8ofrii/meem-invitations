FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay in Egypt?

We accept InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, and Egyptian bank transfers. You pay a 50% deposit to begin work and the remaining 50% only after you review and approve your live invitation link.

How long does the invitation link stay active?

Your invitation link stays online from the day you receive it until 30 days after your celebration date — plenty of time for guests to revisit details, maps, and entry passes.

How do guests receive and open the invitation?

You receive a clean web link that opens instantly inside WhatsApp or any mobile browser on iPhone and Android. With Signature and Royal packages, you can also send personalised links showing each guest’s own name on the cover.

Can I add my own wedding song?

Yes, in our Signature and Royal packages. Simply send us your chosen song and it will play smoothly when guests open the invitation, with a convenient mute/unmute button.

What if my event time or hall changes later?

Date, time, or hall updates before your celebration are completely free — just message us on WhatsApp and your existing link updates automatically without needing to resend it.
