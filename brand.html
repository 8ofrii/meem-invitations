The idea

The Letter ميم

م — Meem embodies the timeless spirit of Mawaddah (مَوَدَّة) and Mahabbah (مَحَبَّة). The logo transforms this letter into an architectural palace arch with an intimate heart at its loop: a doorway, and two souls united in one mark.

Voice

Personal • Modern • Bilingual

Do sound like a friend helping plan the wedding.
Do mix simple Arabic with clear English.
Do be specific: “ready in 48 hours”, “opens with a knock”.
Don’t use pushy sales language or over-promise.
Don’t mix many colours or fonts in one post.

Using the logo

Give it spaceKeep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the heart.
Keep it darkThe logo is designed for dark backgrounds. On light, place it inside its black square.
Don’t alter itNo stretching, recolouring, outlines or shadows.
